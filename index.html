The farm-to-market gap

The same kilo of tomatoes changes hands several times before it reaches a kitchen. Each hop adds cost that the farmer never sees.

Farmer · aggregator · wholesale mandi · retailer

📉

Lower farmer earnings

Prices are set at the point of sale, usually after harvest, when the farmer has the least room to negotiate.

🏷️

Higher consumer prices

Handling, transport and margin at every stage push the shelf price well above what the farm was paid.

🔗

Long supply chain

Three to four intermediaries are common, and each adds a day of travel and a share of the value.

How Farm2Consumer helps

A three-step path from a farm profile to a visible direct-sale opportunity.

01

Farmer registers

A short farm profile captures crops, quantity, current selling price and the method used today.

02

Products get listed

Each crop appears as a listing with quantity, harvest date and the price the farmer is asking.

03

Direct-sale opportunity becomes visible

The calculator shows the earnings difference, so the farmer can judge whether selling direct is worth it.
